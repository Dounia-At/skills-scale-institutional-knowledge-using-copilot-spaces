# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
The Scrum Master / Agile Coach facilitates agile delivery, coaches the team on working practices, and removes impediments so work can flow predictably. They partner closely with the Project Manager to keep execution healthy and sustainable.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Coach teams on agile practices, ceremonies, and continuous improvement
- Identify and unblock delivery bottlenecks and process friction
- Track sprint health, team velocity, and retrospective action items
- Encourage psychological safety and transparent communication
- Support the team in refining workflows and reducing rework

### Goals
- Improve team rhythm and delivery predictability
- Strengthen collaboration and learning across the team
- Reduce unnecessary friction in execution and decision-making

### Typical Communication
- Sprint ceremonies and team huddles
- Impediment logs, coaching sessions, and retrospective follow-ups
- Status updates focused on flow, risk, and team health

### Interacts with existing roles
- Works with Project Managers to keep schedules, blockers, and dependencies visible
- Partners with Product Managers to ensure backlog priorities are clear and achievable
- Supports Developers by helping them focus on sustainable delivery and continuous improvement

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
The QA/Testing Lead owns the quality strategy and validation approach for the project. They work with product, engineering, and project leadership to define acceptance criteria, test coverage, and release readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy across unit, integration, end-to-end, and regression coverage
- Create and maintain test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Coordinate manual QA, validation, and defect triage
- Monitor quality risks and production-impacting issues
- Review release readiness and recommend go/no-go decisions
- Provide quality metrics and reporting to stakeholders

### Goals
- Ensure features meet defined quality standards before release
- Reduce production defects and rework
- Improve confidence in delivery decisions and deployment readiness

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement discussions
- Quality status updates in weekly syncs and release reviews
- Defect reports, validation notes, and release sign-off documentation

### Interacts with existing roles
- Works with Developers to ensure functionality is testable and defects are addressed quickly
- Partners with Product Managers to validate outcomes against acceptance criteria and user value
- Keeps Project Managers and Sponsors informed about quality risks that could affect timelines

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
The Technical Lead / Architect defines the technical direction for the project, guides design decisions, and ensures solutions are maintainable, scalable, and aligned with organizational standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define architecture principles, patterns, and technical standards
- Lead design reviews and technical decision-making discussions
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and mitigation strategies
- Mentor developers and review critical implementation decisions
- Ensure alignment with coding standards, platform constraints, and maintainability goals
- Collaborate with product and project leads on feasibility and trade-offs

### Goals
- Deliver sustainable, scalable, and resilient solutions
- Reduce technical debt and avoid avoidable rework
- Enable the team to make sound engineering trade-offs

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review guidance and mentorship
- Risk escalations during planning, execution, and retrospectives

### Interacts with existing roles
- Works closely with Developers to guide architecture and implementation quality
- Partners with Product Managers to assess feasibility and trade-offs for roadmap decisions
- Supports Project Managers by identifying delivery risks tied to dependencies or technical complexity

---

## DevOps / Release Engineer

### Role Summary
The DevOps / Release Engineer owns deployment pipelines, environment readiness, and release coordination. They enable reliable delivery through automation, observability, and repeatable release processes.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation
- Coordinate environment setup, deployment sequencing, and rollback readiness
- Monitor system health, release metrics, and infrastructure issues
- Support incident response and recovery during release problems
- Maintain security and operational guardrails for production deployments
- Document runbooks and deployment playbooks for the team

### Goals
- Enable frequent, low-risk deployments
- Reduce manual effort and deployment errors
- Improve system reliability and operational visibility

### Typical Communication
- Release planning, pipeline status, and deployment readiness reviews
- Incident response coordination and post-deployment verification updates
- Documentation for operational runbooks and release checklists

### Interacts with existing roles
- Works with Developers to ensure builds, deployments, and rollback plans are reliable
- Supports Project Managers and Sponsors with release timing, risks, and readiness communications
- Collaborates with QA/Testing Lead to verify release quality and smoke-test coverage before production

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
The Stakeholder / Sponsor represents business priorities and strategic intent. They align the initiative with organizational goals, approve scope trade-offs, and champion the project across the broader organization.

### Responsibilities
- Define high-level business objectives and success measures
- Approve scope, timeline, and trade-offs when priorities conflict
- Remove organizational blockers and secure cross-functional support
- Provide executive visibility and stakeholder alignment updates
- Help resolve competing business demands and strategic dependencies

### Goals
- Ensure the project delivers measurable business value
- Maintain strategic alignment and informed decision-making
- Support delivery with organizational sponsorship and resources

### Typical Communication
- Status updates at milestone or executive checkpoints
- Decision meetings on scope, investment, and priority changes
- Escalation of business risks or strategic dependencies

### Interacts with existing roles
- Works with Product Managers to confirm outcome priorities and measure business impact
- Partners with Project Managers to align scope, milestones, and stakeholder communications
- Provides direction and support to Developers, QA, and technical leaders when organizational alignment is needed

---

- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These additional roles help clarify ownership, communication patterns, and accountability across the full project lifecycle.
